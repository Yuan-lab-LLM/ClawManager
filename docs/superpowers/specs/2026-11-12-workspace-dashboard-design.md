Production data rules: no preview names, costs, histories or counts. Authenticated userID determines scope, including for admins visiting personal Workspace. Team tasks and independent tracked runtime tasks retain their native semantics; a completed LLM call is not a completed task. Missing progress is unknown, not guessed. Unread results and pins are scoped to the authenticated user and stable resource/result identifiers. Opening an entry navigates the existing team/instance/session route and never automatically wakes, retries or starts a model call.

Usage is gateway-observed, excludes blocked invocations, and is bounded to the selected interval. Cost comes from recorded actual/estimated amounts; currencies stay separate and no conversion or inferred pricing is allowed. Missing prices/telemetry are distinguishable from known zero. Comparisons use equal-length previous intervals and are unavailable where coverage is insufficient. CPU/memory values are assigned quota, not live utilization. Eligible free slots must reuse scheduler eligibility rules, not capacity minus binding alone. Busy requires explicit task/activity evidence; running and idle are distinct. Historical capacity/state samples start with collection and have null gaps, not fabricated backfills. Annual activity derives from actual tracked task/call records and keeps its own year window.
